The portfolio rule

Most of the money goes where it converts. A little goes where it might.

Every account we run has a core that's proven, usually Google Search, and a small, capped budget for the channel we're testing next. The test line gets real tracking from day one, a limit enforced in code, and a plain verdict after enough spend to know. Then it either earns a bigger share or gets switched off.

YouTube, connected TV and display

Warming a market is a different job from catching a search.

Video and display don't produce a phone call on the first view, and anyone who reports them as if they do is reporting clicks. Where they earn their place is in front of a market that doesn't know you yet: a new location, a seasonal push, a hiring drive. We measure them by what they move, not by what they click.

When it makes sense

A market to warm up

A second location, a rebrand, a season that starts in six weeks, a role you need to fill in four markets at once. Video reaches the people who will search later.

How it's run

Tight geography, capped frequency, clean placements

Delivered inside the radius you serve, capped so nobody sees it ten times, and kept off the placements that exist to burn budget. Your own footage first; stock never.

How it's judged

Branded search and assisted leads, not views

Did searches for your name rise in the markets it ran? Did more leads come through with a view earlier in their path? Those are the numbers, and if they don't move, it stops.

Questions

The honest part, before you spend anything.

Are these real ads inside ChatGPT?

Yes. OpenAI has an advertising platform with its own pixel, conversion events and a server-side Conversions API. We have all three running on golumin.io and we're happy to show you the implementation, including the de-duplication between the browser event and the server one.

Is anyone actually buying from ChatGPT ads yet?

It's early, and the volume is nothing like Google's. We won't pretend otherwise. What we do is measure it properly from day one, as a small capped line, so you find out with a test budget instead of a guess.

Our agency says they "do AI" but can't show us anything.

Ask them for the pixel, the conversion event and the server-side integration. If they can't show you those three things running, they're running a slide deck.

How do you measure TV and display?

By what they move: branded search volume in the markets they ran, and leads that had a view earlier in their path. Never by clicks or views alone. If those numbers don't move after a fair test, the channel is switched off and the budget goes back to what converts.

What if the answer is that we shouldn't spend here?

Then we'll say so on the call, and the audit will tell you where the money should go instead. We run Google, Microsoft, Meta and ChatGPT, so we have no reason to push you into the newest channel.
