Trust & approvals

What we can do, what we can't, and who signs.

Handing anyone access to your ad accounts and your website is a real risk, and "we have human oversight" isn't an answer. Here's the mechanism, in plain English.

Never without a person

  • Apply a change to a live ad account without a named approver
  • Publish or alter a live page without a named editor
  • Exceed a spend limit, even with an approval behind it
  • Create a campaign that starts unpaused
  • Move, sell, or train a third-party model on your business data
  • Change your account ownership, billing, or who has access

Runs on a schedule, unsupervised

  • Read your accounts and your site to find problems
  • Research what buyers in your market are asking
  • Draft a change, with its evidence and the money at stake
  • Flag a page that has gone stale, thin, or broken
  • Surface a conversion-tracking failure the moment it appears
  • Write up what it found, whether or not anyone acts on it
The detail

The answers we'd want if it were our account.

Account ownership

Your Google, Microsoft, Meta and ChatGPT ad accounts stay in your name, with you as owner. We're granted access, and you can revoke it from your own settings without losing a day of history. Same for the domain, the phone numbers, and the content.

Data access

We ask for read-only access to start, on the ad accounts and analytics. Write access comes only after onboarding, and only for what you've agreed to. We don't need, and don't ask for, access to billing, payroll, or customer records beyond the lead data you route to us.

How the software uses your data

Your account data is used to do your work: inspect, research, draft. It isn't sold, shared with other clients, or used to train a third-party model. Which AI model runs a given job is an implementation detail we change when a better one ships. The approval gate doesn't change with it.

Human approvals

Every consequential change is approved by a named person at GoLumin before it applies. Approvers don't approve their own proposals. The name, the timestamp and the original reasoning stay attached to the change.

Spend limits

Limits are enforced in code, not by policy. A proposal over the per-change limit is blocked and shown to you as blocked. An approval doesn't override it. You set the limits during onboarding and can lower them at any time.

Audit history

Applied changes, rejected proposals and blocked ones all stay on the record, with the evidence that was attached at the time. Clients who want to see the queue get access to their own account's history. Most prefer the weekly email.

Pause and leaving

One switch pauses everything on your account, instantly. If you leave, the ad accounts are already yours, the content is portable, and the URLs are yours. We don't hold anything hostage as leverage on a renewal.

Retention

We keep lead and change records for as long as you're a client and for a reasonable period afterwards so the history stays intact. Ask and we'll delete what's ours to delete. The formal version is in the privacy policy.
