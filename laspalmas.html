Where most of it goes wrong

You don't need a marketing department. You need the phone to ring.

“I'm paying someone and I can't tell you what it does.”

A monthly invoice, a PDF of charts, and no answer to the only question that matters: how many customers came from this, and what did each one cost?

“My website looks fine and nobody contacts me.”

Looking fine and converting are different jobs. A site built to describe your business won't out-perform a page built to get one specific person to act.

“I tried Google Ads and burned through the budget.”

Almost always the same two causes: the ads showed for searches that were never going to buy, and the clicks landed on a homepage with nine other places to go.

“I don't know what I don't know.”

That's fair, and it's exactly what the audit is for. You get the findings in writing, in plain English, and you're welcome to hand them to someone else.

Questions

The things people ask on the field.

I run a small local business. Am I too small for this?

Most of the businesses in the case studies on this page are owner-run companies in one or two towns. The audit is useful at any size. If the honest answer turns out to be that you shouldn't be spending on ads yet, we'll tell you that and show you why.

What's the catch with the audit?

There isn't one, and the plan is yours in writing at the end of the call. The catch, if you want to call it that, is that we think you'll read it and conclude we're worth hiring. If you read it and hand it to your current agency instead, that's a fine outcome and it happens.

What does it actually cost if I do hire you?

A monthly fee based on what you spend on ads and whether we also run your website. We quote it after the audit, once we've seen what's really in the account rather than guessed. There's no setup fee and no contract.

I already have someone doing my marketing.

Then use it as a second opinion. It's read-only access, nothing changes in your account, and plenty of people go through this and stay exactly where they are. Knowing what your current spend is doing is worth an hour either way.

I don't have a website yet. Is that a problem?

No. We build sites, and that's half of what the banner says. If you're starting from nothing we'll tell you what to build first and what it should cost, and the landing page that comes with an ads engagement is often the whole thing a service business needs to start.

Is the landing page included?

Yes. It's built during onboarding for every new client and there's no separate charge. On its own that build is $2,500. It goes on your domain, in your name, and it stays yours if you stop working with us.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Google, Microsoft, Meta and ChatGPT accounts stay in your name and you can revoke our access at any time without losing the history. We don't run clients out of our own account.

Is there a contract?

No. We work month to month.
