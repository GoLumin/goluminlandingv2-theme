Teamwork

Three services, one team, one record. Nobody here gets to optimise their own number at another person's expense. The whole point of the company is that the hand-offs don't leak.

Initiative

Nobody will hand you a ticket queue. You'll find the problem, write up what you'd do, and go.

Discipline

The software proposes, a named person approves. That only works if the person signing is genuinely careful. Accuracy, follow-through, and saying no to a plausible-looking bad idea.

Room to grow

Early team, flat structure, no layer to wait behind. The people who join now define how their function works and then lead it.

Hiring process

You apply

A conversation

Something real

Decision

An answer either way, with a reason. Compensation discussed openly against what you'd own.
