BioFuse · IV therapy and wellness clinics · Michigan and Idaho

The same website, tracked properly, twenty times cheaper.

The problem

For ten of the previous agency's final twelve months, the booking flow had no tracking on it at all. About $75,000 went out the door in a year, and the monthly reports counted page views as customers.

What we did

Rebuilt twelve overlapping campaigns into eight with one job each, fenced the spend in so it stopped paying for the wrong searches, put real tracking on the booking flow, and split the four markets so each clinic reports on its own.

What happened

In the ten weeks the old tracking existed it recorded 21 bookings against about $17,500 of spend. In our first 103 days the same website recorded 568 bookings at $42 each.

Questions

The things people ask on the first call.

If yours isn't here, email a person. We'd rather answer an awkward question before you sign than after.

Who is this for?

Businesses already investing in marketing, or ready to, with an owner who can take the calls a working campaign produces. Most of our clients are a single location or a handful of them, in categories where a phone call or a form is the sale. If you're earlier than that, the audit will say so honestly and tell you what to do first.

What does the audit involve?

Read-only access to your site and your accounts. We go through them, write up what we find with the money attached, and walk you through it live on your screen. If we're a fit, we quote the engagement. If we're not, you still leave knowing exactly what's wrong and what to fix first.

What does it cost?

A monthly fee based on your ad spend and whether we run your site, quoted after the audit, once we know what's actually there rather than what we guessed. There's no contract. We work month to month, and we're not the cheapest option, because the people doing the work are the people you talk to.

I already have someone doing my marketing.

The audit works the same way, and we never contact your current agency. Ask them for the change history and the search-terms report. If nobody can produce a list of what changed, when, and who approved it, that's the answer.

Can the software change my ads on its own?

No. The software reads your accounts every day and proposes changes. Every change that touches your money waits for a named person at GoLumin, who can't approve their own proposal. Spend limits are hard limits in code and can't be overridden, even by an approver.

I don't have a website yet. Is that a problem?

No. We build sites, and every new advertising client gets a landing page built for their ads at no charge. On your domain, in your name, and it stays yours if you ever leave.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Google, Microsoft, Meta and ChatGPT accounts stay in your name, and you can revoke our access any time without losing the history. We don't run clients out of our own account.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. If we're not making the phone ring, you shouldn't have to wait out a term to say so.
