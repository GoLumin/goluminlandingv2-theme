Questions

Before you fill in the form.

Is the landing page included?

Yes. It's built as part of onboarding for every new client, with no separate charge. On its own that build is $2,500. It goes on your domain, in your name, and it stays yours if you stop working with us.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Google, Microsoft, Meta and ChatGPT accounts stay in your name, and you can revoke our access at any time without losing the history. We don't run clients out of our own account.

Can the software change my ads on its own?

No. It reads the account continuously and proposes changes; every change that touches your money waits for a named person at GoLumin, who can't approve their own proposal. Spend limits are hard limits in software.

Is there a contract?

No. We work month to month.

How is it priced?

A monthly fee based on ad spend and whether we run your site. It's quoted after the audit, once we know what's actually there rather than what we guessed.

What if we already have an agency?

The audit works the same way: read-only access, a person walks you through what they found, and you keep it. Plenty of people use it as a second opinion and stay where they are.
